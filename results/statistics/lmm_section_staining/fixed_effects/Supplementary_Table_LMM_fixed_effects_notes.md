# Supplementary Table: LMM fixed-effect coefficients

Suggested title: Fixed-effect estimates from the linear mixed-effects models.

Model: value ~ Cy * iPTH + root + (1 | mouse). Models were fitted by restricted maximum likelihood.
Degrees of freedom and coefficient t-tests used the Kenward-Roger approximation.
Cy and iPTH used sum coding (No = +1, Yes = -1); root d was the reference level for the root-region coefficients.
The coefficient p-values are nominal model-coefficient tests and were not multiplicity-adjusted. Planned contrast p-values are reported separately using the prespecified MVT adjustment.
Under the stated sum coding, positive Cy or iPTH coefficients indicate higher outcomes in the corresponding No condition, averaged over the other treatment factor.
